SOLARIS COUNTY MEDICAL EXAMINER -- PRELIMINARY TOXICOLOGY REPORT
Case ref: ME-2291-14SEP
Subject: Ishaan Varma, M, 52

FINDINGS:
- Estimated time of death: between 22:15 and 23:00 on September 14th,
  consistent with witness discovery at 23:10.
- Gastric contents show recently consumed black tea, approx. 150-200ml.
- Toxicology screen of gastric contents and blood serum is positive for
  a fast-acting sedative compound consistent with a thiopental-derivative
  formulation. Concentration is well above any therapeutic dose and is
  assessed as the proximate cause of death (acute cardiorespiratory
  depression), not a natural cardiac event as first assumed on-scene.
- No signs of struggle or external trauma. Subject was seated at the time
  of collapse, consistent with the tea cup found at his desk.
- The specific compound is not one used in this subject's own recorded
  prescriptions. It matches formulations stocked in laboratory sedative
  supply cabinets for animal-model research use, not consumer medication.

CONCLUSION: Death is reclassified from "unexplained cardiac event" to
"suspected poisoning, method: ingestion via beverage." Recommend cross-
reference against any laboratory controlled-substance inventory logs for
the building.
